About Hamza Islam, real estate consultant

A business graduate who turned a passion for property into a full-time advisory practice in Karachi and Dubai.

My background

I am Hamza Islam, a real estate broker and consultant based in DHA Phase 8, Karachi. For the past two years real estate has been my full-time business, and I have built it on one idea: clients deserve an advisor who puts their goals before the commission.

I specialise in off-plan sales with leading developers such as Emaar, HMR Waterfront, Arkadians and Trilliums, as well as secondary market buying, selling and rentals. Alongside Karachi, I work in the Dubai market, helping clients invest across both cities.

To date I have closed property deals worth more than PKR 1.5 billion in Karachi and AED 50 million in Dubai.
